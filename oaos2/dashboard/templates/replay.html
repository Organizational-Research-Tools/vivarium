The replay plays the saved record back against the time since the run's first event. Press play, drag the timeline, or click the activity strip under it to jump; the strip shows how many messages fell in each stretch of the run, so busy periods are easy to find.

The conversation shows every message as it was sent, one line each with its opening words; the newest is shown in full, and a click keeps any other open. By default the replay moves a message at a time (every 3 or 5 seconds), so a back-and-forth can be read turn by turn, and ◀ ▶| step by hand; stepping follows the kinds and the pair you have chosen. Real time instead moves the clock at the chosen speed. The messages carry their recorded text: notes, posts to the board, handoffs (a work product passed to a role; one sent to several roles in a row is one message), and the task's registered exchanges. The task's exchanges start hidden so the talk reads first; the buttons above the conversation switch each kind on or off, and the graph follows them. Messages appear as the cursor reaches them, newest at the bottom; those before the window are faded. To follow one back-and-forth, click an arrow in the graph or a name in a message: the conversation then keeps the messages between those two roles in both directions. A message says nothing about whether it was read or understood.

The graph draws the messages in the window (the last minute, five minutes, fifteen minutes, or everything so far): an arrow's width counts them, and an arrow is highlighted when one fell in the last tenth of the window. A node is filled while its agent has a model call or a tool call open; that is recorded work, not what the model attends to.
