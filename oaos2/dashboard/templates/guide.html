Vivarium in plain language

How the platform works

OAOS2 tests how roles, information sharing, and decision authority affect AI agents’ work. Each study declares what to vary and what to hold constant, then records the agents’ actions and results.

The whole platform in four moves

Choose task conditions, organizational arrangements, and the controls needed for a fair comparison.

Record

Save model requests, reported responses, actions, files, messages, and submissions.

Compare

Score the work and inspect the process that produced it.

How the evidence is made trustworthy

Keep observations separate from interpretations

Controlled comparison

Tasks, organizations, and experiments are declared separately. Automated checks compare supported controls; researchers must also verify that the materials and measurements test the intended idea.

Host controls execution

The host controls agent actions and sends individual requests to AI services. Containers isolate tool execution; provider credentials stay on the host.

Preserved event record

One writer orders events. Archive checks look for missing predecessors, cycles, sequence gaps, event loss, and invalid endings. Passing these checks establishes internal consistency, not the truth of every research claim.

Rebuildable views

Transcripts, tables, and reports use saved events, settings, model responses, and files. Rebuilding with the same evidence and analysis code gives the same output; saved older reports may reflect older code.

Accepted submissions

Accepted submissions use a controlled host-side path. The task declares their required form and how they complete an outcome. Private scoring materials stay outside participant workspaces; public feedback needs its own disclosure checks.

Separate readiness checks

Simulated checks, checks with real models, research readiness, and permission to spend are separate. Passing one does not establish the others.

Limits of the record: an event log cannot by itself establish expertise, sincerity, or sound reasoning. Researchers need separate measures and evidence for those interpretations.
