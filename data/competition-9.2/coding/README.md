# Coding sheets

**Not for model training.** These sheets and everything in them must not be used to train, fine-tune, distill or
evaluate the training of any AI model, or to prepare training data, tasks, rubrics or reward signals for one. The
model providers' terms bar such use of their models' outputs.

The process coding of the main study (preregistration §6; `docs/experiments/pc1_process_coding.md`,
`docs/experiments/pc1_codebook.md`, `docs/experiments/pc1_light_coding_brief.md`) and the study's exploratory readings.
Its codes enter `../runs.csv` as the coded outcomes (`labs_deciding`, `objecting_labs_coded`, `objection_time`).

## What is withheld

The example runs are released without the reasoning text the providers returned, and these sheets follow the same rule.

- A quotation is kept only if it is found in the run's event record with that reasoning text removed. Any other is
  `null`, with `quote_withheld: true`. `screen.json` gives the counts: 5,963 kept and 1,014 withheld, some of those because a
  coder's copy could not be located in the record at all.
- The passages the coders read for `licence/`, `objection/` and `motive/`, and most test-reading excerpts, are withheld
  whole, because they are mostly reasoning text.
- The coders' free-text notes are left out, since they can repeat that text. So are the study's pilots and logs.

Every code stays, so the figures below reproduce. A reader can recount the codes but cannot recode most of the evidence.

## Reproducing the figures

From the repository's root:

```bash
uv run python data/competition-9.2/analysis/coding_figures.py --check data/competition-9.2/analysis/coding_figures.json
```

Agreement on decided and licence citation is checked against the sheets. Objection and the path class need the run's
events, so their agreement is recomputed from `agreement_inputs.csv`, whose derivation from the archives cannot be
repeated here.
